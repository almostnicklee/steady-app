Steady

A quiet coach for your meetings. It watches your face and listens to your voice, then nudges you the moment a reaction might land badly.

Personal setup

Set up in a minute

  1. Point a camera at your face. Prop your phone beside your laptop, or open Steady in a browser window next to your video call.
  2. Line up with the outline. A 15 second setup learns your normal face and voice.
  3. Just meet. The screen stays navy while you look and sound steady, and warms toward red when something slips, with a short tip like "Long exhale."
  4. Review in a minute. See how the meeting went and tell Steady whether the nudges felt right, so it fits you better next time.

Sensitivity
Nudges

All nudges every time a cue triggers. Big nudges only for big moments. Off gives no nudges during the meeting, only the review afterward.

Meeting view

Full color warms the whole screen toward red and shows the face. Private keeps the screen dark, for when others nearby can see your screen.

Softer, shorter vibration and a quieter chime, for anyone who startles easily.

A hand to your forehead, over your eyes or at your temple, and a clear head shake while you are listening. Signals that happen together across face, body, voice and words give one combined nudge. The first session downloads an 8 MB hand model.

Catches phrases that can sound hostile, blaming, defensive, dismissive or exasperated, like "whatever", "not my fault" or "you always". Everyday words like "seriously" or "come on" count only when your face, body or voice is raised at the same time. Only your own speech is checked, while your mouth is moving. Words are turned into text on this device, checked against a list, and thrown away. Nothing is saved or sent. The first session downloads a 28 MB speech model.

Private by design

Everything is analyzed on this device, in memory. No video or audio is recorded, saved, or uploaded. The mic will hear other people, but voice cues only count while your own mouth is moving. With Listen for sharp words on, your own speech is turned into text on this device, checked against a list of phrases, and thrown away; nothing anyone says is saved or sent. Your session scores and settings are saved on this device. If you turn on Remember my normal, a small profile is saved here too: numbers such as your resting brow reading, your usual pitch and pace, and your normal range for each cue. It holds no audio, video or images, it is encrypted with your sign-in, and turning the switch off deletes it. The app's code and models download from public libraries (jsDelivr and Google); they receive an ordinary web request, never your camera or microphone. The page is locked so it cannot connect anywhere else, which also stops the face library's built-in usage reports.

Support

Steady is a skills coach, not therapy. If strong reactions at work often feel hard to manage, a therapist trained in DBT or CBT, an anger management program, or ADHD care can help. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988 at any time.

Recent sessions

Steady is a self-coaching aid for your own use. It reads visible expressions and voice level, not feelings, and its cues are estimates that will sometimes be wrong. It is not a medical or diagnostic tool. It is not for monitoring or evaluating other people, and no one should be required to use it.
