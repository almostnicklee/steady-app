Steady

A quiet coach for your meetings. It watches your face and listens to your voice, then nudges you the moment a reaction might land badly.

Personal setup

Set up in a minute

  1. Point a camera at your face. Prop your phone beside your laptop, or open Steady in a browser window next to your video call.
  2. Line up with the outline. A 15 second setup learns your normal face and voice.
  3. Just meet. The screen stays navy while you look and sound steady, and warms toward red when something slips.

Sensitivity

Private by design

Everything is analyzed on this device, in memory. No video or audio is recorded, saved, or uploaded. The mic will hear other people, but voice cues only count while your own mouth is moving, and nothing anyone says is transcribed or kept. Your session scores and settings are saved on this device. If you turn on Remember my normal, a small profile is saved here too: numbers such as your resting brow reading, your usual pitch and pace, and your normal range for each cue. It holds no audio, video or images, it is encrypted with your sign-in, and turning the switch off deletes it. The app's code and models download from public libraries (jsDelivr and Google); they receive an ordinary web request, never your camera or microphone. The page is locked so it cannot connect anywhere else, which also stops the face library's built-in usage reports.

Recent sessions

Steady is a self-coaching aid for your own use. It reads visible expressions and voice level, not feelings, and its cues are estimates that will sometimes be wrong. It is not a medical or diagnostic tool, and it is not for monitoring or evaluating other people.
