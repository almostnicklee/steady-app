Steady

A live coach for how you come across in conversation. It watches your face and listens to your voice, then warns you before a reaction lands badly.

How to use it

  1. Prop the phone up facing you, about arm's length away, with your face in good light.
  2. Do the 15 second calibration so it learns your normal face and voice.
  3. Have your conversation. Glance at the score, or switch to Discreet and feel a buzz when something slips.

Privacy

Everything is analyzed on this phone, in memory. No video or audio is recorded, saved, or uploaded. The mic will hear the other person, but voice cues only count while your own mouth is moving. Only session scores are kept, on this phone.

Recent sessions

Steady is a practice aid, not a medical or diagnostic tool. Cues are estimates and will sometimes be wrong.
